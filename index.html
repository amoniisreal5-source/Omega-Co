Frequently asked questions.

Can I invest directly through this website?

No. This website collects enquiries only. Review formal project documents, legal arrangements, regulatory status and risks before making any investment decision. No payment or investment subscription is processed here.

Is the €400 monthly VIP benefit guaranteed?

No. The figures are an illustrative proposed benefit structure. Actual discounts and cashback depend on programme rules and qualifying activity. The illustrative earnings component is not a guaranteed return and requires separate documented terms.

What does the $10,000 vacation package include?

The price is indicative. The destination, duration, number of travellers, flights, hotels, transfers, taxes, insurance and exclusions must be confirmed in a written quotation before booking.

How do I submit my information?

Use the relevant enquiry form. In this initial single-file version, submitting a form prepares an email to management using your device's email application. A secure backend is required for automatic online collection and storage.
